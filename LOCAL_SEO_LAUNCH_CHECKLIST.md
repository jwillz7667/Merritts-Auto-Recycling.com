# Local SEO launch checklist

This checklist includes work outside the codebase. None of these actions guarantees rankings.

## P0: before production switch

- [ ] Confirm the production business name exactly matches “Merritt's Auto Recycling.”
- [ ] Confirm address formatting everywhere: `3106 68th Ave N, Brooklyn Center, MN 55429`.
- [ ] Confirm primary phone everywhere: `763-533-2775`.
- [ ] Confirm Google Business Profile hours are 8:00 AM–8:00 PM every day.
- [ ] Confirm the website field on the Google Business Profile uses the canonical HTTPS origin.
- [ ] Confirm the Google profile link and Maps CID still resolve to the intended entity.
- [ ] Confirm no duplicate or former GBP listing conflicts with the canonical location.
- [ ] Confirm whether customers may visit the address; do not label it walk-in or storefront without approval.
- [ ] Complete every P0 item in `CONTENT_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED.md`.

## Google Business Profile quality

- [ ] Choose the most accurate primary category based on actual operations; do not choose a broader category only for reach.
- [ ] Add only owner-confirmed secondary categories.
- [ ] Align service descriptions with the conservative website wording.
- [ ] Remove unsupported “free,” “same-day,” “no title,” and fixed-price claims.
- [ ] Upload current owner-approved business and vehicle photos.
- [ ] Add an accurate business description without city lists or promotional superlatives.
- [ ] Review profile Q&A and answer factual questions from the business account where appropriate.
- [ ] Check holiday/special hours whenever they differ from the daily schedule.

## Reviews

- [ ] Use the canonical Google review link in post-transaction follow-up.
- [ ] Ask all eligible customers consistently; do not gate requests based on expected sentiment.
- [ ] Do not offer incentives for positive reviews.
- [ ] Respond professionally without revealing vehicle-owner personal information.
- [ ] Document source and permission before republishing any review excerpt on the site.

## Citations and entity consistency

- [ ] Audit major directories for exact name, address, phone, URL, and hours.
- [ ] Correct inconsistent old hours and obsolete URLs.
- [ ] Remove listings for locations that do not exist.
- [ ] Keep the call number consistent; use tracking numbers only through a provider that preserves the canonical number in citation ecosystems.
- [ ] Record login owner, listing URL, date checked, and correction status in an internal citation sheet.

## Search engine tooling

- [ ] Verify a Google Search Console **Domain property** through DNS using a business-controlled Google account.
- [ ] Give ongoing Search Console access through the Users and permissions screen; do not share passwords or commit verification values.
- [ ] Submit `https://merritts-auto-recycling.com/sitemap.xml` once in the Sitemaps report and confirm a successful read.
- [ ] Inspect home, core service, Brooklyn Center, Minneapolis, and one guide URL.
- [ ] Request indexing for the home page and a small set of materially updated canonical pages; do not repeatedly submit unchanged URLs.
- [ ] Review Page Indexing for legacy blog and placemark URLs and confirm they settle on the intended canonical destinations.
- [ ] Verify the domain in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the same sitemap.
- [ ] Monitor crawl errors, canonical selection, Core Web Vitals, security issues, manual actions, and supported enhancement reports.
- [ ] Compare submitted versus indexed sitemap URLs after Google has had time to recrawl; investigate patterns rather than isolated delays.

## Local landing-page governance

- [ ] Keep Brooklyn Center tied to the real business address.
- [ ] Keep Minneapolis wording at call-to-confirm unless stronger proof is supplied.
- [ ] Do not create a Saint Paul page until the owner confirms current service and supplies unique local context.
- [ ] Require owner approval and unique evidence for every future city page.
- [ ] Avoid county/city keyword lists in navigation, footer, or hidden text.

## Post-launch checks

- [ ] Day 0: confirm production canonical, robots, sitemap, schema, contact form, headers, and primary redirects.
- [ ] Day 2–3: check contact-form delivery, Vercel errors, Search Console live tests, and GBP website link.
- [ ] Week 1: review crawl/index changes and redirect requests.
- [ ] Week 2–4: compare qualified calls, text starts, and successful inquiries to baseline, not raw traffic alone.
- [ ] Monthly: verify hours, categories, photos, citations, contact-form delivery, and review links.
