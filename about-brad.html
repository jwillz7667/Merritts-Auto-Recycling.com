About Brad

Brad Emholtz — Owner, Merritt's Auto Recycling

Brad has been buying and recycling junk cars across the Twin Cities since 1988. What started as a one-truck operation in Brooklyn Center is now the trusted name when Minnesotans need a fast, fair, cash offer on a vehicle they no longer want.

How Merritt's works — straight from Brad

"I built Merritt's on one promise: I'll quote you a fair number, I'll tow your junk car away for free, and I'll hand you cash when the truck gets there. That promise hasn't changed in 37 years. If you call the number above, you're talking to me or someone I've personally trained — not a call center."

Why neighbors call Brad first

  • Family-owned since 1988. Same owner, same yard in Brooklyn Center, same phone number — 763-533-2775. We answer it ourselves.
  • Free same-day towing. We cover the entire Twin Cities metro plus the outer suburbs — Anoka, Sherburne, Wright, and Mille Lacs counties — out of one yard.
  • Cash on the spot. No bank holds, no checks that bounce, no "we'll mail you something." When the truck arrives, you get paid.
  • Any condition. Running, not running, missing a title, rolled, parted out — we still buy it. If you've got a vehicle taking up space, we want it.
  • Properly licensed. Merritt's is registered with the State of Minnesota as a licensed motor vehicle recycler. Every car we buy is recycled responsibly.

The vehicles we buy

Cars, trucks, vans, SUVs, motor homes, buses, tractors, farm equipment. Domestic or import. 1980s through the current model year. If it has four wheels and you don't want it anymore, Brad wants to make you an offer.
