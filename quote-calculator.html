    How the calculator works

    No black box. Here's exactly what goes into the number you see above.

    Scrap weight value

    We start from the typical curb weight of your vehicle class and multiply by current scrap-steel pricing for Minnesota yards. Heavier vehicles are worth more in scrap — a fullsize truck nets roughly twice what a compact sedan does.

    Year & demand

    Newer vehicles carry parts demand that pre-2000 cars don't. We add a small bonus for 2006+ vehicles and subtract a touch for pre-1996 cars where parts won't fit anything still on the road.

    Condition bonuses

    Catalytic converters, complete drivetrains, intact wheels, and running engines each add real value. A complete running car can be worth $300+ more than the same car parted out and dragged in on a flatbed.
