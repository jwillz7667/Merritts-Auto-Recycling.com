Free Junk Car Removal in Minneapolis, MN: How It Works

By Brad Emholtz · Published April 8, 2026 · 8 min read

Free junk car removal in Minneapolis means exactly that: $0 out of pocket for the tow, cash paid to you at pickup, same-day or next-day scheduling. No "processing fees" or "tow charges" deducted from the offer. Here is the four-step process, the Minneapolis-specific paperwork, and what to expect when our truck pulls up.

Step 1: Get the quote (5 minutes)

Call 763-533-2775. Brad or our dispatcher will ask:

  • Year, make, model, trim if you know it.
  • Does the engine run? Does the car drive?
  • Is the catalytic converter still attached?
  • Any major missing or damaged parts?
  • Your Minneapolis neighborhood and pickup-access details (driveway, alley, behind a building, on a public street).
  • Do you have the title in hand?

We give you a firm cash quote, in writing if you want it emailed or texted. The quote holds for 30 days. No haggling on arrival — what we quote you on the phone is the cash that lands in your hand when the driver shows up.

Step 2: Schedule the pickup (1 minute)

Same-day pickup is available for calls before 2 PM most Monday-through-Saturday days. Otherwise next-business-day. We work in 2-hour pickup windows, so you do not have to wait around all day for a driver.

Minneapolis-specific scheduling notes:

  • Downtown / Loring Park / Elliot Park — alley pickups need to be scheduled in the 10 AM to 2 PM window to avoid trash-day truck traffic.
  • Northeast Minneapolis — the narrow residential streets and limited parking make some pickups require off-peak scheduling.
  • Uptown / Lyn-Lake — generally easy; widest residential streets in the city.
  • South Minneapolis (Powderhorn, Phillips, Longfellow) — straightforward driveway pickups, plenty of access.
  • North Minneapolis (Hawthorne, Near North, Camden) — we cover all of it; alley access on the side streets is easy in most blocks.

If your car is on a public Minneapolis street (rather than a private driveway), confirm with your block whether overnight parking restrictions might trigger a tow before our pickup. Our standard advice: have the vehicle in a driveway, off-street parking pad, or alley where possible.

Step 3: Get your paperwork ready (10 minutes)

Have these on hand by pickup time:

  • Original or duplicate Minnesota title (or out-of-state equivalent). Signed on the back is fine; we will help you fill in the rest if you have not yet.
  • Government-issued photo ID matching the seller named on the title.
  • License plates removed from the vehicle. Both front and rear. They are yours to keep, transfer, or surrender to DVS. See the plate removal guide.
  • Keys — if you have them. We can still take the vehicle without keys if needed, but having them speeds things up.

If your title is missing, you have time before pickup to get a duplicate — DVS issues them for $7.25 and they arrive in the mail within 7 to 10 business days. See the title requirements guide for the full process. We can schedule pickup for the day your duplicate arrives.

Step 4: The pickup itself (30–60 minutes)

The driver arrives within the 2-hour window. On arrival:

  1. Quick visual confirmation of the vehicle against the phone description.
  2. Title transfer paperwork — you sign the back of the title (buyer name = Merritt's Auto Recycling), driver counter-signs a bill of sale, you keep your copy.
  3. Cash payment, counted in front of you. No deductions, no surprises.
  4. The vehicle is winched onto the flatbed or hooked to the tow rig. We are off your property in 30 to 60 minutes typically.

The "all-cash" guarantee: when you call Merritt's, the price we quote is the cash in your pocket at pickup. We do not deduct a "service fee," a "tow charge," a "processing fee," or anything else. If a yard quotes you $600 and shows up with $50 in deductions, that is a $550 yard.

Why "free removal" is actually free (and not a gimmick)

Some yards advertise "free removal!" and then deduct $40 to $80 in tow charges from the offer. We do not do this for two reasons:

  1. The tow is rolled into our pricing model already. Our scrap-and-parts math includes the cost of dispatching a truck. There is no separate "tow line item" because we are buying the vehicle and the transportation is just our cost of doing business.
  2. Brooklyn Center is 7 miles from downtown Minneapolis. The tow time and fuel cost from our yard to anywhere in the Minneapolis 35W ring is under 30 minutes round-trip. The math works for us, and we do not need to nickel-and-dime sellers to make it work.

FAQ for Minneapolis sellers

What if my car is double-parked behind another vehicle?

Move the front vehicle if you can, or have us pull the junk car onto the street with a winch (we have done it many times in Whittier, Cedar-Riverside, and the older neighborhoods with cramped parking). Mention the access situation when you call so we send the right truck.

What if the car has been parked so long the tires are flat?

Not a problem. Our flatbed winches it on regardless of tire condition.

What if I'm a renter and the car has been sitting on the property for years?

As long as you are the titled owner of the vehicle, we can sell it. If the title is in someone else's name (a prior tenant, etc.), the title transfer process gets complicated. Call us and we will walk you through it.

What neighborhoods do you serve?

All 81 Minneapolis neighborhoods, every ZIP code in the city. From Camden to Powderhorn, Loring Park to Bryn Mawr. If your vehicle is in city limits, we are at your driveway within 24 hours.

Do you pay more for cars in better condition?

Yes — running, complete, intact-catalytic-converter vehicles get the high end of the range. We do not lowball complete vehicles, but the offer scales with what is actually there.
